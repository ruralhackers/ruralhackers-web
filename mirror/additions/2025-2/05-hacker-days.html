Hacker Days 2025: Punto de Agua

From 31 August to 7 September, twenty people working in technology, design and communication, and five children, lived together in Anceu for the first Hacker Days. The challenge came from the village itself: Rosabel and Olga, two neighbours over 60, keep the community water system of Anceu running by hand, reading every meter house by house each quarter, spotting leaks and organising the billing.

In one week we built Punto de Agua, an open source app with a shared register, a maintenance calendar and a library of the neighbours’ knowledge, so that the water no longer depends on the memory of a few people. We presented it to the village on the last day. It is designed to be replicated in the more than 1,500 villages that manage their water as a community.

With TEIMAS, SiteGround, VIKO, Eleven Yellow, NEXT DIGITAL, AdoptaUnJunior and Anceu Coliving. Follow the week day by day, or watch the full documentary.
