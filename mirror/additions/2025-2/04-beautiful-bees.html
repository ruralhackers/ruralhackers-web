Beautiful Bees

In July we launched Beautiful Bees, a programme co-funded by the European Union for young Galician women aged 18 to 29 who want to explore new opportunities in rural areas: practical training, a week at the coliving with everything covered, and the chance to travel to an international meeting in Lithuania.

From 4 to 9 November, 24 young women lived together in Anceu and Fornelos: creativity workshops, group challenges, mentoring, reflection circles, an artistic walk through Anceu, and evenings of cooking and storytelling. Read more.
