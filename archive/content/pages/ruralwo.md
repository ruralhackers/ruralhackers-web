## **A residency program for women to connect with WordPress community**

## We believe that access to knowledge and opportunities can empower more women to take active roles in the WordPress ecosystem, creating a more inclusive and diverse community.

![](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIAAAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAAALAAAAAABAAEAAAIBRAA7)

## **A one month experience for women to connect with WordPress in nature**

We are dedicated to fostering strong connections: internally among 5 RuralWO residents to cultivate a sense of belonging and mutual growth, and externally with other WordPress communities to strengthen the overall ecosystem. 

At the same time, we embrace **rural hacking**, using real web design challenges while contributing to rural development.

RuralWO is a blend of learning, impact, and community-building.

## What we offer

-   **Mentoring program:** Engage with the Rural Hackers team and the WordPress community for 4 mentorship, each offering 1 hour of personalized guidance.   
    
-   **Community-driven support:** connect with a network of innovators who can help bring your ideas forward.  
    
-   **Open-Source project:** your project will be developed with an open-source mindset, meaning that it will not only benefit you but also belong to the community, allowing for future improvements and collaborations beyond the residency.  
    
-   **Free Accommodation:** enjoy a shared, comfortable living space at [Anceu Coliving](http://anceu.com), where creativity and collaboration thrive.  
    
-   **Financial aid:** a grant of 500€ to support your living expenses and help you focus fully on your project.
