## Hacker Days

**Hacker Days** is a hands-on, collaborative event organized by **[Rural Hackers](https://ruralhackers.com/)** that brings together technologists, designers, and rural enthusiasts to co-create innovative solutions for pressing challenges in rural communities. Over the course of **one week**, participants work in teams to develop functional prototypes—such as apps, tools, or systems—that address specific local needs, like water management, biodiversity monitoring, or digital inclusion. The event emphasizes **community involvement**, ensuring that solutions are practical, sustainable, and rooted in the realities of rural life. The next edition of Hacker Days will take place from **September 1 to 7, 2025**, at **[Anceu Coliving](https://anceu.com)** in Galicia, Spain, focusing on creating a digital solution to simplify water consumption tracking for the local water association. This event is open to anyone passionate about using technology and creativity to make a tangible impact in rural areas.

## Key points of Hacker Days

![](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIAAAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAAALAAAAAABAAEAAAIBRAA7)

### Focus on Real Problems

Hacker Days tackles tangible issues faced by rural areas, such as **community water management**, **biodiversity monitoring**, or **digital inclusion**.  
  

![](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIAAAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAAALAAAAAABAAEAAAIBRAA7)

### Collaborative Environment

Participants work in teams, combining their skills in **technology, design, and community engagement** to prototype solutions.

![](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIAAAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAAALAAAAAABAAEAAAIBRAA7)

### Hands-On Prototyping

Over the course of a week, participants develop **functional prototypes** that can be tested and implemented in the community.  
  
  

![](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIAAAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAAALAAAAAABAAEAAAIBRAA7)

### Community-Centered

Local residents, like **Rogelio** and **Rosabel**, actively participate, ensuring solutions are practical and rooted in the community’s reality.  
  
  

![](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIAAAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAAALAAAAAABAAEAAAIBRAA7)

### Open source

All solutions are developed as **open-source projects**, allowing other rural communities to adapt and use them.
