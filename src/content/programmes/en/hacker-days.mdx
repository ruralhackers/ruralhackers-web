    <h1 class="section-title">Hacker Days</h1>
    <Prose>
      **Hacker Days** is a hands-on, collaborative event organized by **Rural Hackers**
      that brings together technologists, designers, and rural enthusiasts to co-create
      innovative solutions for pressing challenges in rural communities. Over the course
      of **one week**, participants work in teams to develop functional prototypes — such
      as apps, tools, or systems — that address specific local needs, like water
      management, biodiversity monitoring, or digital inclusion. The event emphasizes
      **community involvement**, ensuring that solutions are practical, sustainable, and
      rooted in the realities of rural life. The next edition of Hacker Days will take
      place from **September 1 to 7, 2025**, at [Anceu Coliving](https://anceu.com) in
      Galicia, Spain, focusing on creating a digital solution to simplify water
      consumption tracking for the local water association. This event is open to anyone
      passionate about using technology and creativity to make a tangible impact in rural
      areas.
    </Prose>
