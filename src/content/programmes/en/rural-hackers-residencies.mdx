    <Prose>
      Our goal is simple but radical: **to generate local impact through creative and
      technological projects that emerge from deep dialogue with Anceu, its people,
      landscape, history, and rhythms.**

      We believe rural settings aren’t just peaceful backdrops, they’re **active
      collaborators**. The quiet mornings, shared meals, forest walks, and conversations
      with the local community spark ideas that cities often drown out. Slowness becomes
      strategy. Isolation becomes focus. Community becomes co-author.

      Your work might live on a forest path, in the main square, inside Casa do Pobo, or
      along a stone wall. What matters is that it’s **seen, used, or experienced by the
      people who call Anceu home**.
    </Prose>

<Section background="accent-5" width="wide" space={50}>
  <h2 class="statement">Past Rural Hackers Residencies</h2>
  <Prose>
    These projects didn’t just happen _in_ Anceu — they happened _with_ Anceu village
    community.
  </Prose>

  <div class="cards three past">
    <div class="card">
      <Figure src={pastCami} alt="Artwork installed along a path in Anceu" />
      <h3>Galicia Ilustrada</h3>
      <Prose size="small">
        [Cam](https://www.camstass.com/) from Belgium immersed herself in the local culture,
        conducting interviews with villagers. This information inspired her to create three
        pieces of art, now installed along the paths of Anceu.
      </Prose>
    </div>
    <div class="card">
      <Figure src={pastRogelio} alt="A lakeside installation made of recycled wood" />
      <h3>A tribute to our neighbour Rogelio</h3>
      <Prose size="small">
        [Shona](https://www.instagram.com/reels/DKVEuaatE6p/), from New York, created a
        lakeside installation using recycled wood and the forest as her canvas. The piece
        honors Rogelio, a neighbor who supports us greatly and carries deep knowledge of
        the land.
      </Prose>
    </div>
    <div class="card">
      <Figure src={pastRaices} alt="Musicians leading a rhythm workshop" />
      <h3>Raices project</h3>
      <Prose size="small">
        [Hugo and Alessandro](https://www.instagram.com/p/DIg23zpKkWj/) from Cuba and Italy,
        musicians from Madrid’s Reina Sofía School led workshops on Cuban and Italian
        rhythms and closed with a live concert.
      </Prose>
    </div>
    <div class="card">
      <Figure src={pastSeen} alt="A painted portrait of a villager" />
      <h3>The Gift of being Seen</h3>
      <Prose size="small">
        [Kristinka](https://www.instagram.com/kristinka.lazar._.atelier/) from Croatia
        painted portraits of 80 local residents during [2024](/progress/2024/). Her goal was
        to go beyond appearances and capture each person’s unique story, history, and
        character.
      </Prose>
    </div>
    <div class="card">
      <Figure src={pastMery} alt="An installation of native plants from the valley" />
      <h3>Where arts meets nature</h3>
      <Prose size="small">
        [Mery](https://www.behance.net/mariajaboska) from Poland embarked on an inner journey
        to discover the local flora. Gathering knowledge from villagers about native plants,
        she created an installation that showcases the botanical richness of the area.
      </Prose>
    </div>
    <div class="card">
      <Figure src={pastMagazine} alt="A copy of the Rural Hackers Magazine" />
      <h3>Rural Hackers Magazine</h3>
      <Prose size="small">
        [Cora and Feli](https://www.instagram.com/p/DHQqE7LMkQN/) from Germany immersed
        themselves in local culture, collecting stories and exploring the impact of our
        projects. The magazine reflects that deep community connection — full of warmth and
        hospitality.
      </Prose>
    </div>
    <div class="card">
      <Figure src={pastReframe} alt="A 3D-printed phone holder mounted as a capture point" />
      <h3>Reframe Cam</h3>
      <Prose size="small">
        Ovi from Romania installed capture points with 3D-printed phone holders so villagers
        can take photos and upload them via QR codes to a shared server. This helps us track
        environmental change over time.
      </Prose>
    </div>
    <div class="card">
      <Figure src={pastRaquel} alt="Fabric dyed with materials gathered from the forest" />
      <h3>Natural Colors of the Forest</h3>
      <Prose size="small">
        [Raquel](https://www.instagram.com/reels/DIwz-EnNlAZ/), from Portugal, explored
        natural dyeing with materials gathered from the forest. Also, she led workshops at
        Casa do Pobo. The residency culminated in a collective artwork, celebrating nature
        and tradition.
      </Prose>
    </div>
    <div class="card">
      <Figure src={pastChano} alt="A page from the Asi e Nito comic" />
      <h3>Asi e Nito Comic</h3>
      <Prose size="small">
        [Chano](https://www.instagram.com/p/C8U0L3vsBZo/), an artist from Galicia, created a
        comic inspired by the village of Anceu. The story follows two mischievous aliens who
        land on Earth and start stirring up chaos among the neighbors of a small rural
        village.
      </Prose>
    </div>
    <div class="card">
      <Figure src={pastTextile} alt="A textile installation among the trees" />
      <h3>Textile art in nature</h3>
      <Prose size="small">
        Shona, from New York, created an installation deep in the forest, using recycled
        textiles. The piece is a tribute to **Se**, one of the people who most actively
        supports the cultural and social life of the village.
      </Prose>
    </div>
    <div class="card">
      <Figure src={pastLotte} alt="A textile piece made during the residency" />
      <h3>Upcycling and Sustainability Advocate</h3>
      <Prose size="small">
        [Lotte](https://www.instagram.com/atelier_luuu/) from the Netherlands connected with
        local artists. She hosted sessions with villagers on creativity. These exchanges
        shaped her process. Her final textile piece reflects the inspiration gathered from
        the community.
      </Prose>
    </div>
    <div class="card">
      <Figure src={pastMural} alt="A mural of Galician icons painted in Ventín" />
      <h3>A mural for Ventin village</h3>
      <Prose size="small">
        [Shona](https://www.instagram.com/reels/DLVLHsQIVF_/) from America created a mural
        which celebrates Galician icons — from hórreos and torques to petroglyphs and the
        Galician cow — and ended with a shared community feast.
      </Prose>
    </div>
  </div>
</Section>
