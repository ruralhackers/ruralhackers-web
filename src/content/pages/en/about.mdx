<Section space={70}>
  # About Rural Hackers

  <Prose font="display" size="x-large">
    A movement for the revitalization of **rural life** //
  </Prose>

  <Prose>
    Rural Hackers is an international organization dedicated to fostering innovation
    and creativity at the intersection of technology, rural living and art. We provide
    a platform for interdisciplinary collaboration, empowering people to challenge
    traditional thinking and create groundbreaking projects that inspire positive change.

    We are based in Anceu, in Ponte Caldelas, Galicia. We build through the power of
    the local villagers of Anceu and our international community.
  </Prose>
</Section>

<Section background="accent-5" space={70}>
  ## Why here

  <Prose>
    Rural areas of Galicia are becoming severely depopulated. This jeopardizes a way of
    living, endangers a whole tradition, and disconnects us from our roots. We envision
    technology, arts and creativity as twisters: they can bring steamy change to rural
    areas and help build new ecosystems.
  </Prose>
</Section>

<Section space={70}>
  ## How we work
