  # Privacy Policy

  <Prose>
    This site is a static website. It has no accounts, no logins and no comments, and
    it sets no cookies of its own.
  </Prose>

  ## When you send us a form

  <Prose>
    The contact form and the Hacker Days form submit to Netlify, which hosts this
    site. Netlify stores the fields you fill in — your name, your email address and
    your message — and notifies us so we can reply. We use that information to answer
    you, and for nothing else.
  </Prose>

  ## Analytics

  <Prose>
    We use Google Analytics to understand how many people visit and which pages they
    read. Google sets its own cookies for this and processes the data on its own terms.
  </Prose>

  ## Embedded content from other sites

  <Prose>
    Some pages link to or embed content hosted elsewhere — for example YouTube videos
    or files on Google Drive. Those services behave as if you had visited them
    directly, and may collect data about you under their own policies.
  </Prose>

  ## Getting in touch about your data

  <Prose>
    Write to us through the [contact form](/contact/) if you want to know what we hold
    about you, or want it deleted.
  </Prose>
